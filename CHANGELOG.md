# Changelog

Every version of Card Barcodes and what it changed, newest first. This file is generated from `changelog.json` by `node scripts/generate-changelog.ts`: edit that, not this. The app shows the same list in Settings > What's New.

## 1.4 (in preparation)

### New

- Card Barcodes is now in Spanish, German, Japanese, French and Arabic, and follows your iPhone's language.
- Turn off iCloud Sync in Settings to keep your cards on this device only, and delete the copy already in iCloud.
- Share a card as a link someone can add to their own Card Barcodes, or as a picture.
- Search your cards, and sort them by last viewed, your own order or the nearest place.
- Keep notes with a card, such as when its points reset. They show on the card, and search finds them.
- Give each card an icon, from 76 in 16 groups, shown on its tile and on the card.
- Add a card from a photo, a screenshot or a PDF of its barcode, such as an e-ticket.
- A card with several places lists the five nearest and how far away each is. Tap one to open it in Maps.
- Choose whether places open in Apple Maps or Google Maps, in Settings. Tap the place at the top of a card to open it there.
- Show the card for where you are in full above your other cards, with a new switch in Settings.
- Show distances in kilometres or miles, or let them follow your iPhone's settings, with a new choice in Settings.
- Find a place by searching for a shop, a street, a town or a postcode. Places are named from the map, and you can change the name.
- Change a place you have added to a card.
- Import cards from a JSON file, as well as by pasting.

### Improved

- The tabs at the bottom of the screen stay in view as you scroll.
- The camera focuses on a card held close. Tap to focus, or turn on the torch in low light.
- Your cards are laid out in a grid, two across on iPhone and four on iPad. Press and hold a card to edit or delete it, or delete it from its edit screen.

### Fixed

- Opening a card no longer plays a haptic, which it only did the first time.
- Dragging cards that all fit on the screen no longer hides the bar at the top and brings it back.
- When your iPhone has been left unused for a while, the Home Screen widget keeps showing the card for where you last were, instead of switching to your first card.

## 1.3 (5 November 2026)

### New

- A Home Screen widget shows the barcode of the card for where you are, or one you choose.
- Give each card a colour from 23, or pick any colour you like.
- Add the places you use a card, from the map or by postcode, and see how far away they are.
- Arrive at one of a card's places and a notification reminds you, and opens the card when you tap it.
- Add where you are to a card straight from the card, with Add current location.
- Turn arrival notifications off for one card when you edit it, or for every card in Settings.
- Pinch to zoom when scanning a barcode.
- Copy a card's number with the button beside it.
- See what changed in each version in Settings > What's New.

### Improved

- Cards show their full colour in the list, with names you can read on any colour.
- Card screens have a new design, with the name and number on white panels.
- A new app icon shows a barcode card behind a QR code card.
- VoiceOver spells out a card's number, and buttons in light mode use a deeper pink that is easier to read.
- The Cards tab keeps your place when you switch to Settings and back.

### Fixed

- New cards go to the end of the list, not the top.
- A card needs a name and a code to save, and two cards cannot share a code.
- Importing skips cards you already have, and adds the rest to the end of the list in the order they were exported.
- The scanner says when the camera is off or missing, instead of showing a black box.
- Messages say "1 card", not "1 cards".

## 1.1 (20 June 2026)

### New

- Use Card Barcodes on iPad, in portrait or landscape.
- Your cards sync between your iPhone and iPad through iCloud.
- Turn Brightness Boost on or off from the card screen.

### Improved

- Add a card with one button: the scanner sits at the top of the form and fills in the code and barcode type.
- Delete All Cards is greyed out when there are no cards to delete.

## 1.0 (26 December 2025)

### New

- Scan a card's barcode with the camera, or type its number in.
- Keep cards with QR, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Aztec or PDF417 barcodes.
- Open a card and the screen brightens, so it scans first time at the till.
- Name your cards, drag them into any order and swipe to delete one.
- Export your cards as JSON and import them again.
- Card Barcodes works in light and dark mode.
