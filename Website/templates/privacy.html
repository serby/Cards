Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short

Card Barcodes collects no personal data. I have no servers, no analytics and no advertising, so I never see your cards, your location or how you use the app.

Data I collect

None. The app does not send your data to me or to anyone else, and it has no third-party SDKs that collect information.

Where your cards are stored

Your cards are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own iCloud account to your other devices, using Apple's CloudKit private database. That data belongs to your Apple Account: Apple stores it under its own privacy policy, and I cannot see or access it.

  • A card holds its name, its barcode number and type, its colour and any places you add to it
  • Your device's security (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode) protects it
  • Deleting a card deletes it on all your devices
  • You can export your cards to a file, and the app only does that when you ask

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, the app uses your location on your device to:

  • Show how far away each card's places are
  • Pick the nearest card for the Home Screen widget
  • Start the map at where you are when you add a place

Your current location never leaves your device. The places you add to a card are stored and synced with the card, as above.

Arrival notifications are scheduled with iOS, which watches for you reaching a card's places and shows the notification. They can be turned off in the app's Settings.

Maps and postcodes

The map and postcode search use Apple Maps. When you look at the map or search for a postcode, your device asks Apple's map service for that area. Apple's privacy policy covers those requests.

Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan barcodes while you are adding a card. Camera images are processed on your device and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your device. No notification service of mine is involved.

No analytics or advertising

The app has no analytics and no adverts. I do not know how many people use it, which features they use or what cards they keep, and I will never sell data because I have none to sell.
