Card Barcodes keeps your loyalty, membership and gym cards on your iPhone, ready to scan at the till.

SCAN A CARD IN SECONDS
Point the camera at a card's barcode and Card Barcodes reads it. It supports QR codes, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Aztec and PDF417. If a card has no barcode, type its number in.

READY AT THE TILL
Open a card and the screen brightens, so the scanner reads it first time. The number sits under the barcode for when it needs typing in.

YOUR NEAREST CARD ON THE HOME SCREEN
Add the widget and it shows the barcode of the card for where you are, or a card you choose. Tap it to open the card.

ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN WHEN YOU ARRIVE
Add the places you use a card, from the map or by postcode. When you get there, a notification with its barcode appears on your Lock Screen. The list shows how far away each card's places are, and you can add where you are straight from a card.

EVERY CARD IN ITS OWN COLOUR
Choose from 23 colours or pick any colour you like. Text stays readable on all of them.

ON YOUR IPHONE AND IPAD
Your cards sync through your own iCloud account, so they are on your iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. There is no account to create.

PRIVATE
There are no ads or analytics, and nothing tracks you. Your current location is only used on your device. Export your cards to a file whenever you want.

FOR EVERYONE
Card Barcodes works with VoiceOver, larger text sizes and dark mode.
