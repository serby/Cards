Card Barcodes needs no account or sign-in.

To add a card by scanning, open https://cards.serby.net/demo.html on another screen. It has a sample EAN-13 barcode and a sample QR code. In the app, tap the scan button at the top of the list and point the camera at either code, then tap Save.

To add a card from a picture instead, tap Choose Photo or Choose File on the same screen and pick an image or a PDF with a barcode in it.

To see a card's places and distances, open a card, tap Edit, then Add Location, and add a place on the map or by searching for a shop, a street, a postcode or a ZIP code. Allow location access when asked. The list then shows how far away the card's places are.

Arrival notifications: with notifications allowed, iOS shows a notification when you arrive at one of its places, and tapping it opens the card. They can be turned off in Settings > Arrival Notifications.

Widget: on the Home Screen, long-press, tap Edit > Add Widget, and choose Card Barcodes. It shows the card nearest to you, or one chosen by long-pressing the widget and tapping Edit Widget.
